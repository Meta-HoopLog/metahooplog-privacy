Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026
Operated by: Meta-HoopLog Operations Office (Japan)

Meta-HoopLog ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This policy applies to both the iPhone version (App Store) and the Android version (Google Play). Where the two versions handle something differently, we say which version it applies to.

Our basic approach
The App does not send the data you create to any external server. Your game records, form analysis results, videos you record and profile photos are all stored only on your own device. There is no account to create and no login.

1. Information we use and why

The App uses the following information, and all of it is processed on your device only.

  • Camera, and videos/photos on your device: Used for Shot Form Analysis and Dribble Analysis. Body-pose detection runs on your device, using Apple's Vision framework on iPhone and Google's ML Kit on Android. Videos you record for analysis are never sent outside your device. Recorded videos and any profile photo you choose are stored only on your device.
  • Motion sensors and physical activity: Used to estimate steps, distance and calories burned in your practice log.
  • Microphone: iPhone only. Used to record audio when you record a video. The Android version does not record audio and does not ask for microphone permission.
  • Scorebook data (game records, player stats, fouls, etc.): Stored only on your device. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Analysis results (launch angle, speed, ratings): Stored only on your device so you can compare them with past results. They are not sent anywhere.

2. Information sent outside the App

All user data created or entered in the App (saved games, recorded videos, analysis results, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never sent to an external server.

However, the App shows ads, and uses the following third-party advertising SDK to do so. To deliver ads, information such as a device identifier and IP address may be sent automatically to the ad provider's servers.

Ad service used: Google AdMob

  • Because the App may be used by children, every ad request is marked as directed to children and as coming from a user under the age of consent, and only non-personalized ads rated for general audiences are shown, regardless of the user's age.
  • On iPhone, the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not ask for tracking permission.
  • On Android, the Advertising ID may be used for ad delivery. You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings.
  • How Google handles this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

On Android, the App also uses Google Play's in-app review feature to ask for a rating. The App does not receive any of your personal information through this feature.

You can choose to export your score data and share it with others using the share sheet, email or other means. This sharing happens only when you do it yourself. The App never sends it out automatically.

4. Sharing with third parties

Except for the automatic transmission for advertising described in Section 2, the App never provides, sells or shares your personal information with any third party.

5. Children under 13

If a child under 13 uses the App, a parent or guardian's consent is required. The parent or guardian should review and confirm on the consent screen in the App.

Because the App is designed with children in mind:

  • We do not collect children's personal information on our servers. We do not have any servers that store user data.
  • Ads are limited as described in Section 2 (marked as directed to children, non-personalized, general-audience content), regardless of the user's age.
  • A parent or guardian can delete all of a child's data at any time by deleting the App from the device (see Section 6), and can contact us with any questions (see Section 8).

6. Deleting your data

Deleting (uninstalling) the App removes all data stored on your device.

On Android, you can also delete all data from Settings → Apps → Meta-HoopLog → Storage → Clear data.

Please note that the App's data is stored only on your device. It cannot be carried over when you change devices or reinstall the App. Game data can be moved manually using the App's export feature.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not store your data on any server, all of the App's data is under your control on your own device, and you can delete it at any time as described in Section 6. For information processed by Google AdMob, please see Google's privacy policy linked in Section 2. If you have any questions, please contact us.
